Swerve Research Technologies Pvt Ltd (“Swerve”, “we”, “us”), a company incorporated in India and based in Pune, Maharashtra, operates the Swerve platform at swerve.wtf. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. It is intended to align with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number.
  • Portfolio data — the holdings, tickers, quantities, and related inputs you enter or upload to compute your portfolio NAV.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment provider (Razorpay). We do not store your full card or bank details; we retain only transaction records and usage-credit balances.
  • Usage data — credits consumed, wallet transactions, and feature usage.
  • Technical data — session tokens stored in your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in, and basic log data.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the service — authenticate you, compute NAV, run analysis tools, and maintain your account.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions and usage credits.
  • To send NAV/report emails you have explicitly opted into (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To provide support, prevent abuse, and improve the platform.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Consent and legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you provide when you create an account and use the service. You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the platform, under appropriate safeguards:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and hosting.
  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • Resend — transactional and subscription emails.
  • Render / cloud infrastructure — application hosting and compute.

Some of these providers process data on servers located outside India. By using the service you consent to such cross-border processing, subject to applicable law.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account data and associated records. Anonymised or aggregated information (including exit-survey responses, with identifiers removed) may be retained for analytics and legal compliance.

6. Your rights

Subject to the DPDP Act, you have the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to grievance redressal. You can exercise account deletion directly from your profile page, or contact us for other requests.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The service is intended only for individuals aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
